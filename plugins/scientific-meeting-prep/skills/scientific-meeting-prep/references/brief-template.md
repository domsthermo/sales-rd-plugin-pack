## Compression rules

For a meeting starting within one hour:

- keep the first screen focused on the objective, posture, known-versus-unknown split, and opening;
- use no more than eight general questions;
- include only techniques that affect the stated customer decision;
- prefer one useful product-fit sentence over a long catalog list;
- keep public company background to facts that change the conversation;
- put secondary sources at the end of the relevant section.
